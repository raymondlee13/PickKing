# PickKing Scan & Build surface: Operate mode

Scope: templates/page.html + static/style.css + static/app.js (also login/register/home, which share style.css). One visitor: Raymond, before lock, laptop at a desk or phone on the couch in the evening.

History: rejected in order were an LED line board and a betting-slip receipt (both gimmicky and hard to read), then a clean generic app ("looks AI generated", no character). The user then chose "Broadcast" from real screenshots of three character options. This is recorded in PRODUCT.md as a brand commitment: character through type and color, not theme costume.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The board looks like a game broadcast's score graphics. Personality comes from big condensed uppercase type, a navy/red/gold palette and slanted score-bug tags. It refuses both metaphor costumes and the generic rounded-pastel app default.

OWN-WORLD: A navy field (#0a1330) with navy surfaces. The red header bar (#d8232a) has a 4px gold rule. Gold (#ffc629) is the one action color, for picks and commits. Green/red are used only for More/Less, goblin/demon and the margin. Big Shoulders Display 800–900 is for the wordmark, names, lines, grades and headings; Barlow is for readable body text. Grades are slanted tags (skewX -10deg): S green, A gold, B blue. Corners are near-square (2–3px).

STORY: Raymond reads the board like a stat graphic: grade tag, big name, big line, then margin. He taps gold checks to add legs, the entry card tallies them against break-even, and he logs with the gold button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The red header bar carries the italic PICKKING wordmark (KING in gold) and a gold-keyed Scan/Track switch. Below it is the game strip, with a red slanted sport tag and the matchup in big caps. Two columns follow. On the left: a sticky filter row, then the board under a red top rule, with gold column heads and rows of slanted grade tag, big caps name, big line figure, and a shared margin axis. On the right: the entry card, with a red header and a gold rule, sticky within its column. On phone it becomes a bottom sheet with a scrim and grab handle.

FORM: A character option ("Broadcast") chosen from three screenshot previews after the user rejected the category standard as generic; seed key 15bee3ed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
